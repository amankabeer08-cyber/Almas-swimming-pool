Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. Find answers to common queries below.

Do I need to book in advance?
Yes, booking in advance is highly recommended to secure your preferred slot and prevent disappointments.
Can beginners swim?
Yes, beginners are welcome! However, they must swim accompanied by another person or under coach supervision for safety.
Do you provide swimming coaches?
Yes, swimming coaching is provided in collaboration with a partner academy named Almas Sports Academy.
Is parking available?
Yes, we have dedicated, spacious parking spots free of charge for all visitors.
